About Wharton Aerospace

Founded in 2004, Wharton Aerospace has built a network of more than 2,000 titans of the aerospace and defense industry over two decades. Twice a year, members of that network gather at Wharton Aerospace and Wharton Aerospace Ventures, long a bedrock of the industry.
